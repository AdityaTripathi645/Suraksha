The challenge

Intense rainfall can trigger landslides, flooding and road blockages. Communities need timely, local warnings.

Our solution

Map-based risk monitoring, rainfall indicators, citizen photo reports, alerts and safer-route awareness.

This is a prototype, not an operational emergency service.

Key features

  • Live risk mapZone-wise risk at a glance
  • Rainfall & forecastCurrent and expected rain
  • Citizen hazard reportSend a photo and location
  • Alerts & safer routingWarnings and open roads

Citizen app

Residents see current conditions and nearby alerts, and report a landslide, flood or blocked road in a few taps.

How it works

One flow from rain on the ground to a safer route for residents.

  1. Collect dataRainfall and sensor readings feed the system.
  2. Score the riskAn explainable score ranks each zone: Low, Moderate, High or Critical.
  3. Raise alertsHigh-risk zones trigger warnings for authorities and residents.
  4. Verify reportsCitizens send photo reports and field officers verify them.
  5. Suggest a safer routeBlocked roads lead to an alternative route recommendation.

Project details

A disaster-intelligence prototype for landslide and flood early warning in Northeast India. The pilot area is Kamrup Metropolitan, Assam. It has two parts: a command center for district authorities and field teams, and a mobile-first app for citizens.

Current status

  • Citizen app with hazard reporting, offline mode, alerts and safety tips is built.

  • Prototype only. Not an operational emergency service.
